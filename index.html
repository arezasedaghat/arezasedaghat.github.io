About

I hold an M.Sc. in Chemical Engineering, in the Modeling, Simulation, and Control group, from Sharif University of Technology (Tehran, Iran). I joined the program in September 2022 and graduated in January 2025. My thesis, “Kinetic modeling and dynamic simulation of an integrated hydrocracking process using SLB Symmetry process simulator, VBA, VB.Net, and Python,” was supervised by Prof. Mahmoud Reza Pishvaie. Before that, I started my B.Sc. in Chemical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology in 2018 and completed it in 2022. My B.Sc. project, “Dynamic simulation and control of an integrated CDU-VDU process using Aspen Plus simulator,” was also carried out under the supervision of Prof. Pishvaie.

Research interests

  • Dynamic modeling and simulation
  • Optimization and control
  • Application of AI in chemical engineering
